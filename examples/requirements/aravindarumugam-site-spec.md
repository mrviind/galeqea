# aravindarumugam.com: Site Requirements

## 1. Roles

- A **Visitor** may browse all public pages without signing in.

## 2. Navigation & pages

- The site must have a Home, About, Blog, Contact, and Products page.
- The Products page shall list exactly two products: SIFT and SIFT-Board.
- Every page's primary navigation links must resolve without a 404.

## 3. Accessibility

- Every page must meet WCAG 2.2 AA, including a text contrast ratio of at least 4.5:1.
- All images must have descriptive alt text.

## 4. Forms

- The Contact page must have a working contact form that accepts a name, an email, and a message.
- The email field is required and must match a standard email format.

## 5. Performance & SEO

- Each page must load within 3 seconds at the 95th percentile.
- Every page must have a unique title and a meta description.
